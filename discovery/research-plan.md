# Discovery Research Plan

## Objective

Validate the highest-friction parts of the corporate travel request-to-book and post-booking experience before committing to an MVP scope.

## Research methods

- Semi-structured interviews with travellers, arrangers, approvers and travel operations.
- Workflow walkthroughs using anonymised or synthetic examples.
- Review of policy documents and approval matrices.
- Analysis of request, approval, booking and support timestamps where access is authorised.
- Usability testing of low-fidelity request and approval prototypes.

## Recruitment

Include a mix of frequent and occasional travellers, different organisation sizes, travel arrangers, approvers and travel operations roles. Avoid treating one company's policy model as universal.

## Interview prompts

1. Tell me about the last business trip you arranged or booked.
2. How did you know which policy rules applied?
3. What information was needed before approval?
4. What happened when a request was outside policy?
5. How did you know the booking was confirmed?
6. What was difficult about changing or cancelling a trip?
7. Which travel reports do you use and what decisions do they support?

## Outputs

- Validated personas and role permissions.
- Current-state journey and friction points.
- Policy and approval rule inventory.
- Supplier and operational exception taxonomy.
- Prioritised opportunity backlog.
- Evidence-backed MVP recommendation.

## Data handling

Use consented research. Remove personal identifiers from notes and examples. Do not upload employee travel records, passport details, payment information or confidential policy documents into this public repository.
