# Corporate Travel Journey Map

| Stage | Traveller / arranger action | Platform responsibility | Potential friction | Product opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Configure | Employee profile and policy context established | Apply role, entity, budget and policy configuration | Incorrect profile or missing cost centre | Guided profile validation |
| Request | Enter one or more destinations, dates, trip purpose and travellers | Validate ordered travel legs and required fields | Complex multi-city data entry | Guided multi-leg itinerary builder |
| International context | Review destination, local time and currency information | Display local time zones, currency context and approved travel reminders | Confusing date boundaries or exchange rates | Clear time-zone and conversion labels |
| Policy check | Review policy guidance for trip and segments | Evaluate policy and explain outcome by leg | Opaque or inconsistent policy result | Rule-level explanation and compliant alternatives |
| Approval | Submit request and monitor decision | Route to approver / delegate | Delays and missing context | Clear status, reminders and escalation |
| Search | Compare flight / hotel options for each leg | Query supported suppliers and display policy labels | Inconsistent content or stale prices | Normalised options and revalidation |
| Book | Confirm selected options | Submit booking and track component supplier responses | Timeout or partial confirmation | Reliable component-level status and reconciliation |
| Travel / mobile | Access itinerary while away from desktop | Provide mobile trip view, alerts and supported service actions | Connectivity, stale status or notification overload | Secure mobile trip workspace and freshness indicators |
| Service | Request change or cancellation | Apply supplier terms and track case | Unclear refund / change status | Guided servicing and ownership |
| Reconcile / review | Finance and travel managers review spend | Present actual, committed, forecast and budget views | Fragmented financial data | Forecast variance and policy insight drill-down |

## Moments that matter

- Understanding why a fare or hotel is out of policy.
- Knowing whether approval is complete.
- Knowing whether the supplier actually confirmed each trip component.
- Understanding local times, currencies and multi-leg connections.
- Accessing accurate itinerary and service status on mobile.
- Understanding forecast assumptions and recurring policy exceptions.
- Seeing the status and owner of an amendment, cancellation or refund.
