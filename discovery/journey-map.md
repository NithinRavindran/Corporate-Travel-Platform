# Corporate Travel Journey Map

| Stage | Traveller / arranger action | Platform responsibility | Potential friction | Product opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Configure | Employee profile and policy context established | Apply role, entity and policy configuration | Incorrect profile or missing cost centre | Guided profile validation |
| Request | Enter origin, destination, dates, purpose and travellers | Validate required fields | Repeated data entry | Saved preferences and contextual defaults |
| Policy check | Review policy guidance | Evaluate rules and explain outcome | Opaque policy result | Show rule, reason and next action |
| Approval | Submit request and monitor decision | Route to approver / delegate | Delays and missing context | Clear status, reminders and escalation |
| Search | Compare flight / hotel options | Query supported suppliers and display policy labels | Inconsistent content or stale prices | Normalised options and revalidation |
| Book | Confirm selected options | Submit booking and track supplier response | Timeout or partial confirmation | Reliable lifecycle and reconciliation |
| Travel | Access itinerary and updates | Surface confirmed itinerary and supported alerts | Outdated itinerary | Timeline and notification preferences |
| Service | Request change or cancellation | Apply supplier terms and track case | Unclear refund / change status | Guided servicing and ownership |
| Reconcile | Review trip and spend | Provide structured booking data | Missing allocation or duplicate records | Finance-ready reporting |

## Moments that matter

- Understanding why a fare or hotel is out of policy.
- Knowing whether approval is complete.
- Knowing whether the supplier actually confirmed the booking.
- Understanding the cost and conditions before committing.
- Seeing the status and owner of an amendment, cancellation or refund.
