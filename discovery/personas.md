# Product Personas

These are working persona hypotheses. Validate roles, needs and decision authority through interviews with real users.

## 1. Employee / Traveller

**Goals**
- Find suitable travel options quickly.
- Understand what is allowed by company policy.
- Receive clear approval and booking status.
- Access itinerary and request changes.

**Pain points to investigate**
- Policy rules are difficult to interpret.
- Approval status is unclear.
- Supplier confirmation and ticketing states are confusing.
- Changes require contacting multiple teams.

**Product needs**
- Guided trip request.
- Policy labels with explanations.
- Clear pending / approved / confirmed states.
- Itinerary and support access.

## 2. Travel Arranger

**Goals**
- Create and manage trips for multiple employees.
- Compare practical options within policy.
- Track pending approvals and supplier exceptions.

**Product needs**
- Delegated access with explicit permissions.
- Traveller selection and profile reuse.
- Multi-traveller request handling where supported.
- Search, booking and servicing visibility.

## 3. Approver / Budget Owner

**Goals**
- Make timely decisions with appropriate financial and policy context.
- Understand exceptions and business justification.

**Product needs**
- Consolidated request summary.
- Policy status and exception reason.
- Itinerary and estimated cost.
- Approve, reject or request clarification with audit history.

## 4. Corporate Travel Administrator

**Goals**
- Configure travel policies and approval hierarchies.
- Manage entities, departments, cost centres and user roles.
- Monitor compliance and exceptions.

**Product needs**
- Versioned policy configuration.
- Effective dates and rule priority.
- Test / preview capability before publishing.
- Access controls and audit logs.

## 5. Travel Operations Agent

**Goals**
- Resolve booking, ticketing, amendment and cancellation issues.
- Coordinate with suppliers and travellers.

**Product needs**
- Booking timeline and supplier references.
- Error and exception queue.
- Ownership, notes and next action.
- Safe retry and reconciliation controls.

## 6. Finance / Procurement Analyst

**Goals**
- Understand travel spend, supplier usage and budget allocation.
- Reconcile bookings with financial systems.

**Product needs**
- Consistent cost-centre data.
- Booking and cancellation records.
- Export / integration controls.
- Definitions for spend and compliance reports.
