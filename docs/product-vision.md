# Product Vision

## Vision statement

Enable organisations to manage business travel through one connected experience that helps employees and travel arrangers plan trips confidently, helps approvers apply travel policies consistently, and gives travel and finance teams reliable visibility into bookings and spend.

## Target customers

- Small and mid-sized organisations centralising employee travel.
- Large enterprises with multiple entities, departments and approval hierarchies.
- Travel management companies serving corporate accounts.
- Employees, travel arrangers, approvers, travel desk and finance stakeholders.

## Value proposition

| Audience | Value |
|---|---|
| Traveller | Find suitable options, understand policy and manage trip details |
| Travel arranger | Book and service travel for authorised employees |
| Approver | Review requests with policy, itinerary and cost context |
| Travel manager | Configure policy and monitor compliance / exceptions |
| Finance | Improve cost allocation, reporting and reconciliation |
| Operations | Resolve supplier and booking issues with traceable workflows |

## Strategic pillars

1. **Policy-aware travel shopping:** present relevant options with transparent policy context.
2. **Controlled approvals:** configurable, auditable workflows that minimise avoidable delays.
3. **Reliable booking and servicing:** consistent lifecycle states across supplier integrations.
4. **Corporate visibility:** actionable insights into spend, compliance and service quality.
5. **Secure enterprise operations:** role-based permissions, privacy and auditability.

## Non-goals for an initial MVP

- Replacing the organisation's complete ERP or expense platform.
- Supporting every supplier, market and fare type at launch.
- Automating exceptional approval decisions without configured controls.
- Guaranteeing supplier inventory or prices beyond supplier validity.
- Providing emergency travel assistance without a defined operating model and support capability.

## Product assumptions to validate

- Employees and arrangers need policy guidance before booking.
- Approval rules differ by entity, department, role, destination and trip type.
- Supplier booking states can be normalised while retaining source-specific details.
- Finance teams need reliable cost-centre and booking data.
