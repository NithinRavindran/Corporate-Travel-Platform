# Outcome-based Product Roadmap

This is a sequence of product capabilities, not a delivery-date commitment. Sequence depends on customer discovery, supplier access, technical dependencies and operating model.

| Horizon | Outcome | Candidate capabilities | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Establish secure corporate context | Identity, traveller profiles, entities, roles, policy configuration | Access and policy scenarios validated |
| Core workflow | Make requests and approvals traceable | Trip request, policy outcome, approval hierarchy, audit history | End-to-end request scenarios pass |
| Booking experience | Support reliable corporate booking | Flight / hotel search, offer display, revalidation, supplier booking status | Booking and reconciliation tests pass |
| Trip operations | Improve servicing visibility | Itinerary, change / cancel requests, service case tracking | Operational UAT and support readiness |
| Business insight | Improve travel programme visibility | Policy, spend, booking and service analytics | Metric definitions and data quality accepted |
| Optimisation | Reduce avoidable friction | Policy insights, proactive alerts, advanced reporting | Evidence-based experiment outcomes |

## Prioritisation principles
- Resolve trust, correctness and control issues before cosmetic enhancements.
- Prioritise end-to-end journeys over isolated screens.
- Make supplier and enterprise dependencies visible.
- Use evidence and confidence, not stakeholder volume alone.
- Revisit sequencing as customer and operational evidence changes.
