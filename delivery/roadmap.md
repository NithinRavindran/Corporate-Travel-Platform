# Corporate Travel Platform Roadmap

All capabilities in this roadmap are part of the product scope. The horizons describe a sensible delivery sequence and dependency order, not future ideas or exclusions. Actual release timing depends on customer discovery, supplier access, technical dependencies, regulatory review and operating model.

| Horizon | Outcome | Capabilities included | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Establish secure corporate context | Identity, traveller profiles, entities, roles, policy configuration, budget and cost-centre structure | Access, policy and finance reference-data scenarios validated |
| Core request and control | Make requests and approvals traceable | Domestic and international trip requests, multi-city legs, policy evaluation, approval hierarchy, audit history, local time / currency context | Representative single-city, multi-city and international scenarios pass |
| Booking experience | Support reliable corporate booking | Flight / hotel search, offer display, revalidation, supplier booking status, component-level trip itinerary | Booking, partial-outcome and reconciliation tests pass |
| Mobile trip management | Keep travellers informed while travelling | Mobile trip overview, itinerary, booking references, supported service requests, secure notifications and approved low-connectivity access | Mobile usability, permission, notification and device-security tests pass |
| Trip operations | Improve servicing visibility | Itinerary, change / cancel requests, disruption updates, service case tracking and trip timeline | Operational UAT and support readiness |
| Spend intelligence | Improve travel programme financial visibility | Actual / committed / forecast / budget views, spend forecasting, variance indicators, policy compliance and exception insights | Forecast definitions, data quality, explainability and finance acceptance |
| Continuous optimisation | Improve programme decisions | Policy insight drill-downs, trend analysis, approval bottleneck analysis and evidence-led policy recommendations | Recommendations validated by policy owners; no unauthorised rule changes |

## Prioritisation principles

- Resolve trust, correctness and control issues before cosmetic enhancements.
- Prioritise end-to-end journeys over isolated screens.
- Make supplier, mobile, finance and enterprise dependencies visible.
- Validate multi-city and international edge cases before broad rollout.
- Treat forecast quality and explainability as launch criteria for spend intelligence.
- Use evidence and confidence, not stakeholder volume alone.
- Revisit sequencing as customer and operational evidence changes.
